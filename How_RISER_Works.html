RISER Hub

How RISER Works

Our work connects the full journey from understanding a problem to developing, testing and translating solutions that can support practical outcomes.

1Challenge 2Observe 3Understand 4Optimise 5Test 6Translate 7Impact
01 · Challenge

Challenge

Every RISER project starts with a real research, industry or operational challenge.

We work with partners to define the problem, understand the requirements and identify the expertise needed to address it.

02 · Observe

Observe

We bring together the data needed to understand the system and its current condition.

Sensor data, asset measurements, battery-management information, operational data and market signals provide the foundation for deeper analysis.

03 · Understand

Understand

We turn data into insight.

Using analytics, modelling, simulation and AI, RISER identifies patterns, risks, anomalies and opportunities for improvement.

04 · Optimise

Optimise

We develop better ways to operate, manage and make decisions.

Optimisation, intelligent control and decision-support methods help identify strategies that improve efficiency, reliability, resilience and overall system performance.

05 · Test

Test

We evaluate promising solutions under realistic conditions.

Simulation, computational analysis, laboratory testing and experimental validation help assess performance, identify limitations and refine the solution.

06 · Translate

Translate

We turn research outcomes into practical tools, methods and solutions.

Depending on the project, these outcomes can take the form of prototypes, analytical platforms, demonstrations, technical methods, decision-support tools and knowledge transfer.

07 · Impact

Impact

The goal is meaningful, real-world impact.

RISER research supports safer batteries, smarter energy systems, stronger resilience, better decisions and more sustainable energy pathways.
