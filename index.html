About RISER

Research built around the future of energy.

The Resilient Intelligent Systems for Clean Energy Research (RISER) Hub at UNSW Canberra brings together multidisciplinary expertise across intelligent systems, optimisation and clean-energy technologies. The Hub focuses on research that can support more adaptive, reliable and sustainable energy systems, while strengthening collaboration across academia, industry and government.
